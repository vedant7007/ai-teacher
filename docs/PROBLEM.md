# Problem

Digital learning gives students two things, and neither of them teaches.

**Pre-recorded lectures** are fixed. They do not know whether you followed the
last three minutes, and they proceed identically whether you understood or not.

**Text chatbots** answer questions. They wait to be asked, they have no plan, and
they cannot tell that your question reveals a misconception rather than a gap.

A real teacher does something different: reads the material, plans a route
through it for *this* student, explains, checks, notices *how* an answer is
wrong, and changes the next explanation because of it.

The brief names three things that are not enough: a question-and-answer chatbot,
a static video, and a talking avatar reading a generated script over plain text.
Every design decision in this project was checked against that sentence.
