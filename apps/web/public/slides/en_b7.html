Experiment Steps:

  • Start with 1 cell, record V and I.
  • Add more cells (2, 3, 4), record V and I each time.
  • Observe how V and I change together.
We'll note down the readings from the ammeter (for current, I) and the voltmeter (for potential difference, V) with one cell. Then, we'll connect two cells in the circuit, increasing the total voltage, and again note the readings. We repeat this process, using three cells and then four cells, each time noting the new current and potential difference. What do you predict will happen to the current as we increase the number of cells, and thus the potential difference?
