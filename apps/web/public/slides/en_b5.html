Quick Recap:

  • Potential Difference (V): The 'push' that moves charges.
  • Electric Current (I): The flow of charges (electrons).
First, let's quickly recap two important terms we've discussed before: potential difference and electric current. Remember, potential difference, often called voltage, is like the 'electrical pressure' or 'push' that makes electric charges move through a conductor. Think of it as the force that drives the current. Electric current, on the other hand, is the actual flow of these charges, usually electrons, through the circuit.
