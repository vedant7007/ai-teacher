Checkpoint 2: Resistance

  • Wire A: R = 5 Ω
  • Wire B: R = 10 Ω
  • Both connected to 12V battery.
  • Which wire has more current? Why?
Now for our second checkpoint, focusing on resistance. You have two copper wires, Wire A and Wire B. Wire A has a resistance of 5 Ohms, and Wire B has a resistance of 10 Ohms. If both wires are connected to the same 12 Volt battery, which wire will allow more current to flow through it, and why? Remember the inverse relationship between current and resistance.
