Recap: Factors Affecting Resistance

  • 1. Length (l): R ∝ l
  • Longer wire = Higher resistance.
Next, we explored the physical factors that influence a conductor's resistance. We found that resistance is directly proportional to its length (l). This means a longer wire will offer more resistance to current flow, making it harder for electrons to pass through. This is an important consideration when planning the layout of electrical wiring.
