Recap: Resistance (R)

  • Property that opposes current flow.
  • SI Unit: Ohm (Ω).
  • 1 Ω = 1 V / 1 A.
  • Higher R = Lower I (for same V).
We then defined resistance (R) as the property of a conductor that opposes the flow of electric current. It's like the internal friction that electrons experience as they move through the material. Its SI unit is the Ohm (Ω), where 1 Ohm equals 1 Volt per 1 Ampere. Remember, higher resistance means less current will flow for the same applied voltage, making it a crucial factor in circuit design. [citations: jesc111-640a680a2407:0009]
