What Affects Resistance?

  • Resistance is not a fixed value.
  • It depends on physical properties of the conductor.
  • We'll explore these factors now.
Now that we understand what resistance is and how it relates to voltage and current, let's explore what factors actually determine how much resistance a conductor has. It's not just a fixed value for all wires; it changes based on a few key physical properties.
