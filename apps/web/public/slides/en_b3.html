Think about a water pipe. If the pipe is very narrow, or if it has many bends and rough insides, water doesn't flow through it as easily, right? It faces more resistance. But if the pipe is wide and smooth, water flows freely. Electricity behaves in a very similar way within wires and components. The properties of the wire affect how easily current flows.
