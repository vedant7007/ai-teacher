Recap: Factors Affecting Resistance

  • 3. Nature of Material: Electrical Resistivity (ρ)
  • SI Unit: Ohm-metre (Ω m).
  • Low ρ = Good conductor; High ρ = Insulator.
Finally, we learned that resistance depends on the nature of the material itself. This intrinsic property is quantified by electrical resistivity (ρ), with units of Ohm-metre (Ω m). Different materials have different resistivities; good conductors have low resistivity, while insulators have very high resistivity. This explains why we choose specific materials for different electrical applications. [citations: jesc111-640a680a2407:0012]
