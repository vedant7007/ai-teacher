To convert this proportionality, V ∝ I, into an equation, we introduce a constant. This constant is called 'R', which stands for resistance. So, Ohm's Law can be written as V = IR. This is one of the most fundamental equations in electricity. It tells us exactly how voltage, current, and resistance are mathematically linked in a circuit. [citations: jesc111-640a680a2407:0009]
