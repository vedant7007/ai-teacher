We can rearrange Ohm's Law to show current: I = V/R. This equation clearly shows that the current (I) through a resistor is inversely proportional to its resistance (R), assuming the potential difference (V) remains constant. This means if the resistance increases, the current will decrease, and vice versa. This inverse relationship is very important for controlling current in circuits.
