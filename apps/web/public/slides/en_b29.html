What We Observe:

  • Different materials give different current readings.
  • Resistance depends on the nature of the material.
  • Some materials are better conductors than others.
You'd definitely notice a different current reading! Copper is a much better conductor than nichrome, so the current would be significantly higher. This observation confirms that the resistance of a conductor also depends on the nature of its material. Different materials have different internal structures, which affect how easily electrons can move through them. This is why we use copper for electrical wires but alloys like nichrome for heating elements. [citations: jesc111-640a680a2407:0011]
