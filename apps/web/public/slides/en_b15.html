What is Resistance (R)?

  • Property of a conductor.
  • Opposes the flow of electric charges.
  • Acts like 'electrical friction'.
So, we've established that 'R' in V = IR stands for resistance. But what is it, really? Electrical resistance is the property of a conductor to oppose or resist the flow of electric charges, or electrons, through it. It's like an internal 'friction' that slows down the electrons as they try to move. Every material has some amount of resistance, even good conductors. [citations: jesc111-640a680a2407:0009]
