Road Width Analogy:

  • Wide highway = Large Area = Low Resistance (Smooth flow)
  • Narrow road = Small Area = High Resistance (Congested flow)
Let's use our road analogy again. Imagine a highway. If the highway is very wide, many cars can travel at the same time, and traffic flows smoothly. This is like a thick wire with a large cross-sectional area, offering low resistance. But if the road is narrow, only a few cars can pass, causing congestion and slowing down the flow. This is like a thin wire with a small cross-sectional area, offering high resistance.
