Doorway Analogy:

  • Narrow doorway = High Resistance = Low Flow (Current)
  • Wide doorway = Low Resistance = High Flow (Current)
To understand this inverse relationship, let's use another everyday example. Imagine a busy doorway (representing the current) in a building. If the doorway is very narrow (high resistance), only a few people can pass through at a time. But if the doorway is wide (low resistance), many more people can pass through easily. So, a wider doorway means less resistance and more flow, just like a lower electrical resistance means more current.
